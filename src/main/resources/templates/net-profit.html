Net profit = net sales minus net COGS, paid operating expenses, net spoilage and finalized transfer losses. Taxes, depreciation, financing and unrecorded costs are excluded: this is not a statutory income statement. Currencies are never combined. Unknown loss costs suppress net profit, never become zero.

Sales/refunds use posting dates; expenses use paid dates and voids their correction dates, all in UTC. Transfer losses belong to the source branch on final receipt. In-transit stock is not an expense. Stock adjustments are not automatically classified as operating losses.
